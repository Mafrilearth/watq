💎 Scientific Metaphor & Optical Philosophy: Why "The Psychometric Prism"?
In physical optics, white light appears uniform to the naked eye until it refracts through an optical prism, deconstructing into a measurable color spectrum. Human personality operates identically: an individual appears monolithic on the surface, but through the Watq cognitive assessment engine, their psychological tendencies are deconstructed with mathematical precision across the 4 primary cognitive axes. The 3 solid facets naturally outline the letter W without delicate wireframe lines, creating an authoritative, singular mark.
